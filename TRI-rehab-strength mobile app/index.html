All three are performed with the knee bent — a straight knee shifts the load onto the gastrocnemius and misses the soleus entirely. Progress from Exercise 1 toward Exercise 3 as symptoms allow.

Key technique reminders

  1. The bent-knee principle: the soleus sits beneath the larger, visible gastrocnemius, and both attach to the Achilles tendon — strengthening it matters for tendon health, not just calf strength.
  2. Straight vs. bent: a straight-knee calf raise trains the gastrocnemius. Only a bent-knee movement isolates and loads the soleus.
  3. Load (seated raise): pick a weight where reps 8–15 are hard but controlled, not shaky.
  4. Full range (step raise): don't let the working knee straighten as your ankle rises — that's when the work quietly shifts back onto the gastrocnemius.
