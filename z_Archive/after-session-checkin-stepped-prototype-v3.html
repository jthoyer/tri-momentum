    Why this shape

    One decision per screen matches the moment of use — driveway, heart rate still up, one thumb. Options get large because nothing competes for the space, and the count replaces scroll length as the signal of how much is left.

    The niggle question is asked third, not eighth. It is the one answer that can force a caution for the whole week, so it is the one answer a stepped flow can least afford to lose to someone closing the app halfway. Everything behind it is refinement; that question is the floor.

    This build is the full-only variant: every question is asked as its own step, every time, so the telemetry below measures the cost of the complete flow rather than a shortened one. A sibling build with a Fast alternative — TrainingPeaks' shorter post-workout ask, run against the same full flow on the same athletes — lives in after-session-checkin-stepped-prototype.html.

    Saving does not end on a receipt. The last screen is the week: the session drops into today and the count moves toward the five-session floor the roll-up needs. Seeing the week become computable is the argument for logging the next one.

    The captured values use the same vocabulary as Prototype 02, so a session logged here feeds the identical D/E/F/N/S computation at week's end — this changes the shape of the capture, not what gets captured.

    Accessibility notes
    • Auto-advance carries a 260 ms delay, a live-region announcement, focus moved to the new heading, and Back that keeps your answer selected — 3.2.2 / 2.2.1. Turn it off with the Auto-advance switch above.
    • Progress is stated as text as well as drawn as a bar; the bar is aria-hidden so it is not announced twice.
    • Targets are 56 px, single column — past 2.5.5 at AAA. Text floor is 14 px throughout, matching the app.
    • The review step is the commit point: nothing is saved until you tap Save (3.3.4). Unanswered questions send you back to the first gap rather than failing silently.
    • The week screen states progress as text, and the logged row carries aria-current plus the words “just logged” — never colour alone (1.4.1).
    • Header and footer are sticky within the flow rather than fixed to the viewport, so the page still reflows and scrolls at 200% zoom (1.4.4 / 1.4.10).
    • Copying the test data announces success or falls back to a selectable text box if the clipboard is unavailable.
    • The starting splash auto-dismisses after 1.5 seconds — under the 5 second threshold SC 2.2.2 exempts — but a tap anywhere, the Continue button, or any key press skips it immediately, and focus lands on it first so it isn't silently missed.
    For testers — how this works

    This is the stepped-flow alternative to Prototype 02's single scrolling form — the full nine-question version, with no Fast shortcut. In the shipped app it fires when a session ends and pulls discipline, intended intensity and duration from your plan; here you declare them so there is something to compute from.

    • Opens on a branded splash for about 1.5 seconds. Tap it, tap Continue, or press any key to skip straight to the flow — this only shows once per page load, not on Log another.
    • Answer end to end. Duration over 45 minutes inserts the fuelling question, so the count in the header changes from 8 to 9 as you go.
    • Three sessions are pre-seeded into the week (Mon–Wed) so the final screen has something to sit in. Your logged session lands on the next open day.
    • Log another starts a fresh session and fills the next day forward.
    • Copy test data puts every timing and answer from this visit on your clipboard as JSON. It sits on the last screen and again under the flow map, so a check-in you gave up on part-way can still be sent in. Do it before you close the tab — reloading clears everything.
    • State resets when you reload — nothing is saved anywhere.

    Prototype only — no accounts, no browser storage, no analytics service. Sessions and step timings live in memory for this visit and clear on reload. Nothing is transmitted anywhere; the one way data leaves this page is Copy test data, which you tap yourself and which puts the JSON on your own clipboard.
