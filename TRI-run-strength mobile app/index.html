Key technique reminders

  1. Balance: Single-leg RDL and reverse lunge both load one leg at a time — keep your hips square to the front, don't let them rotate open.
  2. Load: Pick a dumbbell weight where reps 7–8 of the RDL are hard but controlled, not shaky.
  3. Copenhagen: Hips high, no sagging — this drives the adductor strength that protects the knee under running and pedalling load.
  4. Pace: The jump squat closes every round — land soft and reset your breathing before starting the next round.

Total session time: about 18 minutes.
