CIRCUIT · ~25 MIN

Single-leg strength circuit

0/28

One continuous circuit, four rounds. Move straight through the seven exercises with minimal rest; rest 60–90 seconds between rounds.

Key technique reminders

  1. Balance: Single-leg RDL and reverse lunge both load one leg at a time — keep your hips square to the front, don't let them rotate open.
  2. Load: Pick a dumbbell weight where reps 7–8 of the RDL are hard but controlled, not shaky.
  3. Single-leg squat + jump: Land soft on the same leg you jumped from — if your hip drops or your knee caves on landing, shrink the jump height before adding reps.
  4. Copenhagen: Hips high, no sagging — this drives the adductor strength that protects the knee under running and pedalling load.
  5. Tiptoe walk: Keep a soft bend in the knees throughout — a straight leg shifts the work off the soleus and onto the calf, missing the point of the exercise.

Total session time: about 25 minutes.
