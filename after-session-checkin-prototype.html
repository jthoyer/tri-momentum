    For testers — how this works

    This is a working prototype of the per-session check-in. In the real app it fires automatically when a session ends and pulls the session's discipline, intended intensity and duration from your plan. Here you declare those yourself so there's something to compute from.

    • Time cost is real: three decisioning taps + three feeling taps + an optional note is about 15–20 seconds, not 10. That's deliberate — the extra feeling taps are what let the app compute your structure and state instead of asking you on Sunday.
    • Log several sessions to build up a week, then tap See week's end.
    • State resets when you reload — nothing is saved anywhere.

    Prototype only — no accounts, no data leaves this page, no browser storage. Sessions live in memory for this visit and clear on reload.
